## Overview

Briar Point Campground is an award-winning lakeside destination offering 179 tent and RV sites directly on the banks of Stonewall Jackson Lake. Whether you're bringing your full-size RV with 50-amp service or pitching a tent on the shoreline, Briar Point has the facilities and natural beauty that make it a premier West Virginia campground.

## About Briar Point

Nestled within Stonewall Resort State Park, Briar Point combines modern RV amenities with pristine lakeside camping experiences. The campground is managed by West Virginia State Parks in partnership with the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, ensuring well-maintained facilities and lake management throughout the year.

**Key Facts:**
- 179 total sites (40 50-amp RV, 33 30-amp RV, 5-6 tent)
- Open year-round with seasonal service levels
- Direct Stonewall Jackson Lake access
- Full hookup availability
- Award-winning facilities and maintenance

## Site Options

### RV Sites (Full Hookups)
- **40 sites** with 50-amp electric service
- **33 sites** with 30-amp electric service
- All include electric, water, and sewer connections
- Modern RV pads with gravel surfaces
- Dump station available for waste disposal
- Fresh water fill-up stations

### Tent Sites
- **5-6 lakeside tent sites**
- Waterfront locations with direct beach access
- Table and fire ring provided
- Water spigots nearby
- More secluded camping experience

## Amenities & Facilities

**Comfort & Convenience:**
- Modern restrooms and hot shower facilities
- Laundry facilities (seasonal availability)
- RV dump station with fresh water
- Picnic areas with grills and tables
- Children's playground
- WiFi available in select areas

**Recreation & Water Access:**
- Sandy beach area for swimming
- Direct lake access for fishing
- Boat launch ramps on-site
- Fishing pier areas
- Day-use picnic grounds

## Rates & Reservations

**Peak Season Pricing** (Memorial Day - October 31)
- RV with hookups: $63-75 per night
- Holiday weekends: $75 per night
- Tent sites: $50-60 per night (estimated)

**Off-Season Pricing** (November - May)
- Reduced rates available
- Limited services in winter months
- Call for current pricing and availability

**How to Reserve:**
- Phone: 304-269-8889
- Online: Recreation.gov
- Advance booking recommended for peak season
- Holiday weekends book up quickly

## Fishing at Stonewall Jackson Lake

Stonewall Jackson Lake is a 1,608-acre reservoir known for quality fishing opportunities:

**Fish Species:**
- Largemouth bass
- Smallmouth bass
- Walleye
- Muskie
- Channel catfish
- Crappie and bluegill

**Fishing Tips:**
- WV fishing license required
- Public boat ramps throughout lake
- Shoreline fishing available from campsites
- Marina bait and tackle services nearby

## Water Sports & Recreation

Beyond fishing, Stonewall Jackson Lake offers:

- **Boat Rentals**: Pontoons, fishing boats, jet skis at marina
- **Paddling**: Kayak and canoe rentals
- **Swimming**: Beach area and lake access
- **Boating**: 5 public ramps for launching personal watercraft

## Campground Rules & Policies

**Check-In / Check-Out:**
- Check-in: 2:00 PM
- Check-out: 11:00 AM
- Late checkout subject to additional fees

**Quiet Hours:**
- 10:00 PM - 7:00 AM (no loud music, generators)
- Respect neighbors' camping experience

**Pets:**
- Dogs welcome on leash
- Must not be left unattended
- Owner responsible for cleanup
- Designated pet relief areas

**Campfires:**
- Permitted in site fire rings only
- Follow all posted fire restrictions
- Check seasonal fire advisories

**Length Restrictions:**
- RVs up to 45 feet accommodated at some sites
- Check specific site limits at registration

## Seasons & Best Times to Visit

### Spring (April-May)
- Wildflowers and warm weather returning
- Fishing season ramping up
- Fewer crowds than summer
- Some services may be limited early season

### Summer (June-August)
- Peak season for families
- Perfect weather for water sports
- All facilities open
- Reservations essential for weekends

### Fall (September-October)
- Beautiful foliage around lake
- Cooler temperatures perfect for camping
- Labor Day still busy, September quieter
- Great fishing conditions

### Winter (November-March)
- Year-round camping available
- Limited services and utilities
- Peaceful lake setting
- Winter fishing opportunities
- Best for quiet, solitude-seeking campers
