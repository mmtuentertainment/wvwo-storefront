# Stonewall Jackson Lake WMA - 18,289 Acres of Lewis County Hunting

Stonewall Jackson Lake Wildlife Management Area wraps around the 2,650-acre Stonewall Jackson Lake in Lewis County. Nearly 19,000 acres of mixed hardwood forest on rolling terrain - perfect habitat for deer, turkey, bear, and waterfowl.

One of West Virginia's most accessible WMAs with Class Q designation for hunters with disabilities, plus a 300-yard rifle range right on-site for sighting in before season.

## What Makes It Special

- **18,289 acres** of public hunting land
- **Class Q accessible** - Expanded vehicle access for disabled hunters
- **2,650-acre lake** at the heart of the WMA
- **300-yard rifle range** - Sight in your rifle before the season
- **Stonewall Resort nearby** - Full-service lodging and dining
- **Waterfowl hunting** - Lake coves and backwaters hold ducks

## What to Hunt

| Species | Season | Notes |
|---------|--------|-------|
| White-tailed Deer | Sept 27 - Dec 31 (archery) | Class Q accessible |
| Wild Turkey | April 20 - May 24 (spring) | 2 bearded birds |
| Black Bear | Sept - Dec (check dates) | BG + Bear Damage stamps required |
| Waterfowl | Check WV DNR | Lake coves and backwaters |
| Ruffed Grouse | Oct 18 - Feb 28 | Daily limit: 4 |

## Critical Regulations

- **WV hunting license required** with Class BG stamp for big game
- **Electronic game check** within 24 hours via WVhunt.com
- **400+ square inches blaze orange** during firearms deer season
- **No Sunday hunting** in West Virginia
- **No camping on WMA land** - Use Stonewall Resort or nearby campgrounds
- **Portable tree stands only** - Must be removed after each hunt

## 300-Yard Rifle Range

One of the best public ranges in the area:
- **300-yard distance** - Enough for long-range rifle practice
- **Covered shooting positions** - Shoot rain or shine
- **Free to use** - Just bring your own targets
- **Pre-season sighting** - Get your rifle dialed before opening day
