# Stonewall Jackson Lake

West Virginia's premier bass fishing destination. With 2,650 acres and 82 miles of shoreline, Stonewall Jackson is nationally recognized as the #1 largemouth and smallmouth bass lake in the state. Add walleye, muskie, crappie, and catfish to the mix, and you've got year-round fishing opportunity.

## Why Stonewall Jackson Stands Out

- **#1 Bass Lake in WV** - Recognized by WV DNR and national fishing publications
- **2,650 acres** - Plenty of water and structure to explore
- **82 miles of shoreline** - Irregular V-shape created by West Fork and Skin Creek drainages
- **Diverse species** - Bass, walleye, muskie, crappie, catfish, white bass
- **Trophy potential** - Muskie minimum 52 inches; trophy bass year-round
- **374-slip marina** - Stonewall Resort Marina with full amenities
- **5 public boat ramps** - Access to entire lake

## The Lake

Stonewall Jackson Lake was created in 1988 as a flood control project on the West Fork River. The 95-foot tall concrete dam impounds the water across an irregular V-shaped valley, with the main West Fork River channel and the Skin Creek drainage arm providing distinct fishing environments.

## Fishing by Species

### Largemouth Bass (Primary Target)

Largemouth bass dominate the upper lake and coves. Spring and fall peak action as fish move to shallower structure.

- **Best Season:** Spring (March-May) and Fall (September-November)
- **Techniques:** Topwater early morning/late evening, Texas-rigged worms (4-5 inch), spinnerbaits along shallow cover
- **Regulations:** Daily limit 6 (combined with smallmouth). No minimum size.
- **Where:** Skin Creek drainage arm, shallow coves, shoreline timber

### Smallmouth Bass (Consistent Producer)

Rocky structure and deep ledges harbor excellent smallmouth populations. Available year-round with peak activity spring and fall.

- **Best Season:** Spring and Fall
- **Techniques:** Tube jigs on rocky structure, drop shot rigs, finesse presentations
- **Regulations:** Daily limit 6 (combined with largemouth). No minimum size.
- **Where:** West Fork River channel, dam area, rocky points and ledges

### Walleye (Fall/Winter Action)

Walleye move to deeper structure in fall and winter, providing excellent jigging opportunities.

- **Best Season:** Fall and Winter
- **Techniques:** Vertical jigging near dam, live minnow trailers on jig heads
- **Regulations:** Check with WV DNR District 3 (304-924-6211) for current regulations
- **Where:** Deep ledges and drop-offs near dam, 20-40 feet

### Muskellunge (Trophy Fish)

Stonewall Jackson is a destination muskie lake with a 52-inch minimum size requirement (stricter than most WV lakes). Fall is prime time.

- **Best Season:** Fall (September-November)
- **Techniques:** Large bucktails and jerkbaits, heavy tackle, patience
- **Regulations:** Minimum 52 inches. Daily limit 1. Modified regulations - verify with WVDNR.
- **Where:** West Fork River channel, deep structure near dam
- **Reality Check:** "The fish of 10,000 casts" - muskie fishing requires dedication

### Crappie (Spring Specialist)

Shallow coves in Skin Creek drainage arm provide excellent spring crappie spawn. Brush piles hold spawning fish.

- **Best Season:** Spring (March-May)
- **Techniques:** Small jigs (1/32 oz), live minnows around brush piles and timber
- **Regulations:** Daily limit 30 (combined black and white). No minimum size.
- **Where:** Skin Creek coves, brush piles, shallow flats

### Channel Catfish (Year-Round)

Channel catfish are available year-round, with summer and fall providing best action. Night fishing productive.

- **Best Season:** Summer and Fall
- **Techniques:** Chicken liver, cut shad, stink bait; anchor near structure
- **Regulations:** Daily limit 4. No minimum size.
- **Where:** Deeper holes, points, channel structure

### Other Species

- **Bluegill & Panfish** - Shallow water near vegetation, excellent for kids
- **White Bass** - Spring and fall schooling runs, small jigs and spinners
- **Common Carp** - Spring and summer in shallow areas

## Water Management

Stonewall Jackson is a flood control reservoir with minimal seasonal drawdown - typically only 5 feet in winter. This means boat ramps and fishing access remain excellent year-round. The lake rarely goes completely down like some WV impoundments.

## Best Times to Fish

### Spring (March-May)
Crappie spawn in coves, bass move shallow, water warming. This is when locals hit hard.

### Summer (June-August)
Bass move to deeper structure midday. Topwater in early morning/late evening. Walleye and catfish available.

### Fall (September-November)
Peak season. Bass and walleye feed heavily before winter. Muskie action excellent. Water cooling and fish feeding aggressively.

### Winter (December-February)
Vertical jigging for walleye in deep water. Crappie suspend near structure. Fewer people, often bigger fish.

## Regulations & Safety

**Fishing License:** Required for all anglers 15+. Buy at WV Wild Outdoors (nearby) or online at elicense.wv.gov.

**Muskie (Special Regs):** 52-inch minimum (stricter than other WV lakes). Daily limit 1. Modified regulations apply - verify current rules with WVDNR.

**Bass Regulations:** Daily limit 6 (combined largemouth/smallmouth). No minimum size.

**Boating Safety:** Life jackets required for all. Children 12 and under must wear PFD constantly. Boater safety card required if operator born after Dec 31, 1986.

**Winter Access:** Salmon Run boat ramp is the only year-round launch if other ramps close (though Stonewall Jackson only draws down 5 feet).
