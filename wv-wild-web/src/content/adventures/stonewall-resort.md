# Stonewall Resort State Park

**AAA Four Diamond resort on West Virginia's #1 bass fishing lake**

Stonewall Resort State Park is West Virginia's premier year-round resort destination, combining the natural beauty of Stonewall Jackson Lake with world-class amenities including an Arnold Palmer Signature Golf Course, full-service spa, 374-slip marina, and Adirondack-style lodge. Located just 3 miles off I-79 in Lewis County, the 1,736-acre resort offers everything from championship golf to family fishing adventures.

## Quick Facts

- **Size**: 1,736 acres on Stonewall Jackson Lake (2,650 acres)
- **Rating**: AAA Four Diamond
- **Lodging**: 198 lodge rooms + 10 lakeside cottages + Briar Point Campground (179 sites)
- **Season**: Year-round resort operations
- **Golf**: 18-hole Arnold Palmer Signature Course (par 72, 7,149 yards)
- **Marina**: 374 slips (West Virginia's largest)
- **Contact**: 304-269-7400 | [stonewallresort.com](https://www.stonewallresort.com)

## Golf at Stonewall

The Arnold Palmer Signature Course at Stonewall Resort is consistently ranked among West Virginia's best golf experiences. Designed by Arnold Palmer & Ed Seay in 2002, the championship course features dramatic elevation changes, scenic lakeside holes, and challenging greens.

**Course Details**:
- Par 72, 7,149 yards from championship tees
- #2 America's Best New Courses (Golf Digest 2003)
- Top 10 Palmer Courses You Can Play (Links Magazine 2011)
- Year-round play (weather dependent)
- Professional instruction and club rentals available
- Practice facilities and putting green

**Pro Shop**: 304-269-7400

## Marina & Water Activities

Stonewall's 374-slip marina is West Virginia's largest and offers full-service boat rentals, fuel, bait, and slip rentals on Stonewall Jackson Lake - the state's #1 bass fishing destination.

**Marina Services** (April - Late Fall):
- Pontoon boat rentals (21+ with valid driver's license)
- Fishing boat rentals
- Personal watercraft
- Kayaks, canoes, and stand-up paddleboards
- Fuel dock and bait shop
- Marina hours: 9 AM - 5 PM

**Fishing**:
- West Virginia's #1 bass fishing lake (Game and Fish Magazine)
- Largemouth bass, smallmouth bass, walleye, muskellunge
- Crappie, channel catfish, bluegill
- 82 miles of shoreline to explore
- Catch-and-release program for black bass
- **WV fishing license required (ages 15+)**

**Marina Contact**: 304-269-8895

## Mtn. Laurel Spa

Escape to the full-service Mtn. Laurel Spa for massage therapy, facials, body treatments, and wellness services. Open year-round with reservations recommended.

**Services**:
- Massage therapy (Swedish, deep tissue, hot stone)
- Facials and skin treatments
- Body wraps and scrubs
- Manicures and pedicures
- Couples treatments available

**Spa Reservations**: 304-269-7400

## Lodging Options

**Lodge Rooms** (198 rooms):
- Adirondack-style architecture with lake or mountain views
- Flat-screen TVs, mini-fridges, Wi-Fi
- Balconies available
- Indoor heated pool and hot tub access
- Check-in: 4:00 PM | Check-out: 11:00 AM

**Lakeside Cottages** (10 cottages):
- Sleeps 4-8 guests
- Full kitchens and multiple bedrooms
- Private decks with lake views
- Washer/dryer included
- Perfect for families and longer stays

**Briar Point Campground** (179 sites):
- Lakeside camping with RV electric hook-ups and tent sites
- Modern restrooms and showers
- Boat mooring posts available
- Peak season: $63/night standard, $75/night holidays
- Year-round availability (limited winter services)
- Reservations: [recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov)

## Hiking & Recreation

Explore 16+ miles of trails ranging from easy nature walks to challenging ridge hikes through mixed hardwood forest. The recreation center offers rock climbing, fitness facilities, and equipment rentals.

**Trail Highlights**:
- Lake overlook trails with scenic views
- Wildlife viewing opportunities (deer, turkey, songbirds)
- Interpretive nature trails
- Year-round accessibility

**Recreation Center**:
- Indoor rock climbing wall
- Fitness center
- Kayak/canoe/SUP rentals
- Playground for children
