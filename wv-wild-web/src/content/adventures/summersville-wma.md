# Summersville Lake WMA - 5,982 Acres of Nicholas County Hunting

Summersville Lake Wildlife Management Area wraps around West Virginia's largest lake - the 2,790-acre Summersville Lake in Nicholas County. Nearly 6,000 acres of mixed hardwood forest on steep terrain surrounding the iconic blue waters.

Just 30 minutes from our shop, this is one of the closest WMAs for our customers. Good deer and turkey hunting, plus small game opportunities throughout the season.

## What Makes It Special

- **5,982 acres** of public hunting land
- **2,790-acre lake** - West Virginia's largest
- **Archery trail reopened** - July 2024, back in operation
- **Lake access** - Combine hunting with fishing or camping
- **30 minutes from shop** - Our closest WMA
- **Diverse habitat** - Hardwoods, regenerating cuts, lake edges

## NO BEAR HUNTING IN 2025

**CRITICAL:** Nicholas County has been removed from the 2025 bear season. No bear hunting permitted at Summersville Lake WMA this year. Check WV DNR regulations before planning a bear hunt.

| Species | Season | Notes |

| Ruffed Grouse | Oct 18 - Feb 28 | Daily limit: 4 |
| Gray Squirrel | Sept 10 - Feb 28 | Daily limit: 6 |
| Waterfowl | Check WV DNR | Lake coves and backwaters |
| Mourning Dove | Sept 1 - Oct 30 | Daily limit: 15 |
| Woodcock | Oct 18 - Nov 30 | Daily limit: 3 |

## Critical Regulations

- **WV hunting license required** with Class BG stamp for big game
- **Electronic game check** within 24 hours via WVhunt.com
- **400+ square inches blaze orange** during firearms deer season
- **No Sunday hunting** in West Virginia
- **NO BEAR HUNTING 2025** - Nicholas County removed from bear season
- **Portable tree stands only** - Must be removed after each hunt

## Archery Trail (Reopened July 2024)

The archery trail is back open after maintenance:
- **Practice your shooting** before bow season
- **Various distances** - Work on range estimation
- **Free to use** - Just bring your own equipment
- **Good pre-season prep** - Get dialed in before opening day

From our shop in Birch River:
1. Head south on WV-4
2. Take US-19 South toward Summersville
3. Follow signs to Summersville Lake
4. About 30 minutes total drive time
