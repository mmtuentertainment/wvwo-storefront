## Overview

Stonewall Jackson Lake is a U.S. Army Corps of Engineers (USACE) reservoir located near Weston, West Virginia, in Lewis County. The lake is easily accessible from Interstate 79 and is a major recreation destination for fishing (bass, muskie, crappie), boating, paddling, and camping.

**Key Facts:**
- **Elevation:** 1,825 feet
- **Surface Area:** 2,650 acres
- **Shoreline:** 82 miles
- **Maximum Depth:** 95 feet
- **Nearest Town:** Weston, WV (15 miles)
- **Access:** I-79 Exits 91 (Roanoke) and 96 (Weston)
- **Managed By:** Pittsburgh District, U.S. Army Corps of Engineers
- **Boating:** 5 public boat ramps | 374-slip marina at resort

## PRIMARY USACE CAMPGROUND

### Briar Point Campground (Stonewall Resort State Park)

**Status:** ONLY USACE-managed overnight campground on Stonewall Jackson Lake

#### Location & Access

| **Nearest Towns** | Weston (15 mi) | Clarksburg (35 mi) |

#### Campsite Details

**Total Capacity:**
- **46 Total Sites** (per some sources state "40 RV + 6 tent")
- **RV Sites:** 40 full-hookup (7 with 50-amp service, 33 with 30-amp service)
- **Tent Sites:** 5-6 tent-only sites with deck platforms
- **Mooring Posts:** Available for boat tie-up on first-come, first-serve basis

**Site Configuration:**
- **Maximum RV Length:** 45-50 feet (sources vary; confirm at booking)
- **Site Types:** Mix of pull-through and back-in sites
- **Surface:** Level blacktop pads with picnic tables and fire rings
- **Waterfront Access:** Nearly all sites are on peninsula extending into lake (waterfront camping)

**Amenities (Per Site):**
- Electricity hookup (30-amp or 50-amp)
- Water hookup (all RV sites)
- Sewer hookup (all RV sites)
- Picnic table
- Fire ring with grill
- Ground-level deck (tent sites)
- Mooring post options for boats

## IMPORTANT NOTES FOR DATA COLLECTION

### CRITICAL CLARIFICATIONS

1. **USACE vs. State Park:**
   - Briar Point Campground is USACE-managed (Pittsburgh District)
   - Overlayed with West Virginia State Parks administration (Stonewall Resort State Park)
   - This is the ONLY overnight campground with USACE management on Stonewall Jackson Lake

2. **NOT at Stonewall Jackson Lake:**
   - Bulltown Campground is at **Burnsville Lake** (different lake, ~25 miles away)
   - Burnsville WMA is separate from Stonewall Jackson Lake WMA

3. **Site Capacity Discrepancies:**
   - Various sources report 40-46 RV sites and 5-6 tent sites
   - Total range: 45-46 overnight sites (possible additional sites per resort)
   - Confirm exact count at booking: (304) 269-8889

4. **Hookup Confusion in Sources:**
   - Hipcamp reports: "46 sites (no sewer hookups)"
   - Briar Point official: "full hookups on all RV sites"
   - **Confirmed:** ALL 40 RV sites have full hookups (water, electric, sewer)

5. **State Park vs. USACE Terminology:**
   - The lake is USACE-managed (Corps dam and reservoir)
   - The campground is within Stonewall Resort State Park boundaries
   - Recreation.gov lists it under USACE Pittsburgh District
   - WV State Parks handles some day-to-day operations
