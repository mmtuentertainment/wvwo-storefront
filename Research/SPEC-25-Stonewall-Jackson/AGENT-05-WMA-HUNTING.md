## Stonewall Jackson Lake Wildlife Management Area

### Overview
- **Name**: Stonewall Jackson Lake Wildlife Management Area
- **Management**: West Virginia Division of Natural Resources (WV DNR)
- **District**: Central District (WV DNR)
- **Connection**: Surrounds/adjacent to Stonewall Jackson Lake USACE property

### Hunting Opportunities

#### Permitted Species (Class-Q Hunting)
- **White-tailed Deer** - Primary game species
- **Wild Turkey** - Spring and fall seasons
- **Waterfowl** - Duck and geese hunting opportunities
- ⚠️ **Additional species likely** (bear, grouse, squirrel - verify with DNR)

#### Class-Q Hunting
- **Designation**: Class-Q permits hunting for deer, turkey, and waterfowl
- **Regulations**: Follow statewide WV DNR regulations
- **License Required**: WV hunting license + appropriate stamps/tags

## Facilities & Restrictions

### Shooting Range
- **Type**: 300-yard rifle range
- **Location**: Within the WMA
- **Purpose**: Sighting-in rifles, target practice
- **Access**: Public use (verify hours/fees with DNR)

### Camping Restrictions
- **WMA Camping**: Prohibited in the WMA
- **Alternative**: Campgrounds available at Stonewall Resort State Park
- **Note**: This is common for WV WMAs to protect wildlife habitat
