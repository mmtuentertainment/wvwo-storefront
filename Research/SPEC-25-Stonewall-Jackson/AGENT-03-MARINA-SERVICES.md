## Primary Marina: Stonewall Resort Marina

### Location & Capacity
- **Name**: Marina at Stonewall Resort
- **Slips**: 374 boat slips (20-foot and 30-foot slips available)
- **Address**: Located at Stonewall Resort State Park
- **Phone**: 304-269-8895
- **Season**: Closed for winter - **Reopens April 2, 2026**

### Services Offered

#### Boat Rentals
- **Pontoon Boats**: New models with Bluetooth stereo systems
- **CraigCats**: Three 2-person watercraft available
- **Fishing Boats**: Available for rent
- **Aqua Cycles**: Paddle-powered watercraft
- **Kayaks & Canoes**: Available through Recreation Center
- **SUP (Stand-Up Paddleboards)**: Available through Recreation Center

#### Rental Hours
- **Operating Hours**: 9 AM - 5 PM
- **Weekend**: Friday through Sunday
- **Weekday**: Monday through Thursday
- **Rental Requirements**: Must be 21+ years old with valid driver's license

### Facilities

#### Marina Store & Services
- **Fuel Sales**: Gas station for boats
- **Bait Shop**: Fishing supplies and bait
- **Marina Store**: Snacks, drinks, boating supplies
- **Public Restrooms**: Available at marina
- **Picnic Areas**: Waterfront picnic facilities

#### Boat Launch
- **Public Launch**: Full-service boat launch at marina
- **Additional Ramps**: 4 other public ramps around lake (5 total)
- **Launch Access**: Free public access (verify fees with USACE)
