## Lake Specifications

### Surface Area & Dimensions
- **Surface Area**: 2,650 acres (some sources cite 2,630 acres)
- **Length**: 26 miles
- **Shoreline**: 82 miles
- **Drainage Area**: 102 square miles
- **Shape**: Irregular "V" shape created by West Fork and Skin Creek drainages

### Dam Specifications
- **Type**: Concrete gravity dam with uncontrolled spillway
- **Overall Length**: 620 feet
- **Maximum Height**: 95 feet above stream
- **Concrete Volume**: 107,000 cubic yards (enough to cover a football field with a concrete block nearly five stories high)
- **Sluices**: Five sluices through the dam
- **Year Operational**: 1988 (construction began 1986)

### Water Management
- **Winter Drawdown**: Only 5 feet
- **Purpose**: Flood control project (Pittsburgh District USACE)
- **River**: West Fork River impoundment

### Geographic Context
- **Location**: Lewis County, West Virginia
- **Highway Access**: 3 miles off I-79, 10 miles from Weston
- **Coordinates**: Near 39.0° N, 80.5° W

### Notable Features
- One of 16 flood risk management reservoirs in USACE Pittsburgh District
- 5 public boat ramps around the lake
- 374-slip marina at Stonewall Resort
- Visitor center with dam overlook and hiking trail
