## Primary Campground: Briar Point Campground

### Overview
- **Name**: Briar Point Campground
- **Management**: Part of Stonewall Resort State Park (1,736 acres total)
- **Location**: Lakeside on Stonewall Jackson Lake
- **Sites**: 179 tent and RV sites
- **Season**: Open year-round

### Site Features
- **RV Sites**: Full hook-ups available
- **Tent Sites**: Lakeside locations
- **Setting**: Banks of the lake with waterfront views
- **Award**: "Award-winning" campground designation

### Camping Fees (Peak Season)
- **Memorial Day - End of October**: $63 per night (standard)
- **Holiday Weekends**: $75 per night
- **Off-Season Rates**: Likely reduced (verify with park)

### Amenities
- **Electric Hook-ups**: Available at RV sites
- **Water**: Available at sites
- **Dump Station**: On-site RV services
- **Restrooms/Showers**: Modern facilities
- **Lakeside Access**: Direct access to Stonewall Jackson Lake

### Reservations
- **Booking**: Call (304) 269-8889
- **Online**: Available through Recreation.gov
- **Advance Booking**: Recommended for peak season

## USACE Relationship

### Stonewall Jackson Lake Management
- **USACE Role**: Manages lake as flood control project (Pittsburgh District)
- **State Park**: WV State Parks manages resort and campground facilities
- **Partnership**: USACE provides lake infrastructure, state manages recreation

### USACE Facilities at Lake
- **Visitor Center**: Public restrooms, dam overlook, hiking trail
- **Address**: 1012 Skin Creek Road, Weston, WV 26452
- **Phone**: 304-269-4588
- **Boat Ramps**: 5 public ramps managed by USACE
- **Picnic Areas**: Day-use areas around lake
