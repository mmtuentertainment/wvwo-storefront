# Stonewall Jackson Lake Fishing Research
**Last Updated:** January 10, 2026
**Data Source:** West Virginia Division of Natural Resources (WVDNR) 2024-2025 Regulations

---

## Lake Overview

**Location:** Lewis County, West Virginia
**Size:** 2,650 acres
**Impoundment Year:** 1989
**Operator:** U.S. Army Corps of Engineers
**Notable Status:** #1 bass fishing lake in West Virginia for 10+ years

---

## Fish Species & Regulations

### Largemouth Bass (Black Bass)
| Regulation | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Minimum Size** | One fish 18 inches or longer per day |
| **Daily Creel Limit** | 6 fish total; only 1 may be 18"+ |
| **Special Rule** | Can harvest fish under 18 inches |
| **Tournament Exemption** | DNR-permitted tournament anglers exempt from size restrictions |
| **Best Season** | Spring-early summer (April-June); water temp 55-65°F |

**Fishing Patterns:**
- **Spring:** Spawning in 2-4 feet of water when temps reach 60°F
- **Summer Mornings:** Shallow water fishing most productive
- **Fall:** Topwater over treetops; fish riprap tightly
- **Winter:** Focus on deeper structure where bass congregate

**Recommended Lures:**
- Spinnerbaits
- Plastic worms (green/brown natural colors)
- Crankbaits (shallow around riprap)
- Topwater plugs (fall/spring)
- Jigs and spoons (deep water)
- Worms and jigs around grass

**Fishing Depths:** 4-10 feet near standing timber, riprap, drop-offs

### Trout (Stocked)
| Regulation | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Stocking Location** | West Fork River tailwaters (not main lake) |
| **Stocking Schedule** | Feb-Apr: 2x monthly; May: 1x monthly |
| **License Requirement** | Trout stamp required (Class O or Class O-L) |
| **Best Season** | February through May during active stocking |

**Note:** Trout are stocked in the tailwaters below the dam, not in the main lake body.

## Habitat & Structure

### Key Fishing Structure
- **Submerged Standing Timber:** Abundant since 1989 impoundment; excellent cover
- **Old Roadbeds:** Visible underwater; hold concentrations of fish
- **Riprap (Shoreline):** Rocky areas produce year-round; best in fall
- **Creek Arms:** Multiple backwater areas with varied depth
- **Drop-Offs:** Transition zones where fish stage

### Why Stonewall Jackson Produces Trophy Fish
Since impoundment in 1989, the lake's submerged tree structure and complexity created an ideal ecosystem:
- Exceptional cover allows forage fish to thrive
- Game fish populations reached trophy sizes
- Black bass catch-and-release regulations protect breeding stock
- Deep, clean water supports diverse species

## Best Practices for Success

### Spring/Early Summer (High Success Period)
1. Target spawning areas in 2-4 feet near structure
2. Use plastic worms and topwater plugs
3. Fish early morning and late afternoon
4. Look for bass in shallow water 40-60°F range

### Summer (Heat Period)
1. Move to deeper water (8-15 feet)
2. Fish structure more carefully
3. Use live bait or swimbaits
4. Early morning and evening more productive

### Fall (Aggressive Period)
1. Topwater plugs effective over timber
2. Fish riprap tightly with crankbaits
3. Water temperature drop triggers feeding
4. Extended fishing windows (morning through evening)

### Winter (Deep Water)
1. Focus on deepest structure
2. Slow presentations (jigging, vertical fishing)
3. Use smaller baits and lures
4. Patience required; fewer, quality bites

### General Tips
- **Respect Catch-and-Release:** Smallmouth and spotted bass must be released
- **Check License:** Valid fishing license and trout stamp (if applicable) required
- **Weather Patterns:** Cloud cover and wind often improve fishing
- **Peak Times:** Spring and fall typically most productive overall
