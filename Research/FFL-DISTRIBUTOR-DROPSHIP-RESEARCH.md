# FFL Distributor Dropship Research: "Hooking Up Through Suppliers"

**Research Date:** January 13, 2026
**Context:** Kim (Type 02 FFL - Pawnbroker) mentioned she can "hook up through suppliers" for online sales
**Goal:** Understand what distributor networks Kim can access and how dropshipping works for small FFLs

---

## Executive Summary

**What "hook up through suppliers" means:** Kim can open dealer accounts with major firearms distributors (Davidson's, Lipsey's, Sports South, RSR Group) who will:
1. Give her access to their full catalog (10,000+ SKUs) via online dealer portals
2. Allow her to sell products she doesn't stock
3. Dropship firearms to customer's chosen FFL
4. Dropship accessories/ammo directly to customers

**Key Insight:** Kim never needs to physically handle firearms. Customer browses her website, pays Kim, distributor ships to customer's local FFL. Kim earns margin between dealer cost and retail price.

**Realistic Margins:** 10-30% markup on firearms ($30-$200 per gun), higher margins on accessories/ammo/optics.

## 3. Realistic Profit Margins & Economics

### Firearms Margins (Industry Reality Check)

**New Firearms:**
- **Typical markup: 10-30%** (varies by brand/model)
- **Profit per gun: $30-$200** (depending on price point)
- Industry operates on THIN margins compared to other retail

**Examples from FFL Forums:**
- Distributor cost: 15% below MSRP → Sell at 7% over cost = slim margins
- Popular guns (Ruger 10/22, Remington 870): Big box stores sell below small dealer wholesale cost
- High-volume dealers make $50-75 per gun
- Small dealers make $30-50 per gun on average

**Better Margin Opportunities:**
- **Used guns:** No dealer cost/MSRP → Set your own premium
- **Accessories:** Higher margins than firearms
- **Ammunition:** Good profit source
- **Gunsmithing services:** Premium pricing
- **FFL transfer fees:** Pure profit ($25-50 per transfer)

### Business Model for Small FFLs

**Money Math (Dropship Model):**
- Customer buys Ruger American ($500 retail)
- Kim's dealer cost: $400 (20% margin)
- Kim's profit: $100
- Kim's time: 15 minutes (process order, collect payment, route to distributor)
- **Effective hourly rate: $400/hour** (if it takes 15 min)

**Reality Check:**
- Need volume to make real income (thin margins require scale)
- Used guns, accessories, ammo supplement firearms income
- FFL transfers ($25-50 each) add up with volume

## 4. Home-Based FFL Dropship Business Model (2026)

### Good News: Home-Based FFLs Are Common

**Legal Status:**
- Home-based FFLs are LEGAL as long as not prohibited by local law
- **Majority of U.S. FFLs are home-based** (industry standard)
- ATF permits home-based Federal Firearms License operations

**Requirements:**
- Must not violate local zoning laws
- Must allow ATF compliance inspections at business location
- Must maintain proper records (bound book, A&D records)

**Kim's Type 02 (Pawnbroker) Status:**
- Likely has physical storefront (pawn shop requirement)
- Stronger position than pure home-based FFL for distributor accounts

### Dropship Model Logistics

**How Dropshipping Works with FFLs:**
1. Customer browses Kim's website, selects firearm
2. Customer pays Kim directly
3. **Customer chooses receiving FFL** (their local dealer)
4. Kim places order with distributor
5. **Distributor ships to customer's FFL** (NOT customer's home)
6. Customer picks up at their local FFL, completes 4473
7. Customer's FFL charges transfer fee ($25-50) to customer

**Critical Rule:** Firearms can ONLY ship to licensed FFL dealers, never directly to customer's home.

**Kim's Workload Per Sale:**
- Collect payment (credit card processing)
- Verify customer age/eligibility (basic checks)
- Place order with distributor (online portal)
- Provide customer with tracking info
- Handle customer service questions
- **Total time: 15-30 minutes per sale**

### Distributor Minimums (Potential Barriers)

**Reality Check:**
- Most distributors require $5,000+ initial order for NEW accounts
- Some require minimum annual purchases ($7,500+)
- Home-based FFLs may face scrutiny (less than brick-and-mortar)

**Kim's Advantage:**
- Type 02 (Pawnbroker) implies physical storefront
- Likely already has established business presence
- May already have distributor accounts (should verify)

## 7. Kim's Actual Workload (Per Sale)

### Dropship Firearms Order (15-30 minutes)

**Steps:**
1. Customer places order on website (automated)
2. Payment processing (automated via Stripe/Square)
3. Kim receives order notification (email/dashboard)
4. Kim logs into distributor portal (Davidson's/Lipsey's)
5. Places order with customer's FFL as ship-to address (5 min)
6. Distributor emails tracking info to Kim (automated)
7. Kim forwards tracking to customer (2 min)
8. Customer receives notification from receiving FFL when arrives (automated)

**Potential Issues Kim Handles:**
- Out of stock at distributor (refund or substitute)
- Customer's chosen FFL doesn't respond (help customer find different FFL)
- Shipping delays (communicate with customer)
- Product questions (before sale)

**Total Active Time:** 10-20 minutes per order

---

### Dropship Accessories Order (5-10 minutes)

**Steps:**
1. Customer places order (automated)
2. Payment processing (automated)
3. Kim logs into distributor portal
4. Places order with customer's home address (3 min)
5. Tracking email forwarded (1 min)
