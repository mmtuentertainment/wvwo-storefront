## Getting There: Directions & Coordinates

### From Interstate 79

**Primary Route (Stonewall Resort State Park):**
- **Interstate:** I-79
- **Exit:** 91 (Roanoke)
- **Distance from Exit:** Approximately 3 miles (5 km)
- **Direction:** South of Weston

**Alternative Route (Dam & Tail Waters):**
- **Interstate:** I-79
- **Exit:** 96 (second Weston exit)
- **Access to:** Dam tailwaters and eastern access points

## Emergency Information

### In Case of Emergency

| Situation | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| **Medical Emergency** | Call 911 (limited cell service; signal to shore) |
| **Boating Accident** | Signal DNR patrols; use shore contact |
| **Nearest Hospital** | Weston area (10 miles away) |
| **DNR Non-Emergency** | (304) 269-7463 |

### Safety Considerations

1. **Cell Service:** No reliable service on water; inform someone on shore of trip plans
2. **Weather:** Mountain terrain subject to rapid weather changes
3. **Water Temperature:** Winter/spring water cold (40-60°F); wear life jackets
4. **Life Jackets:** Required by law for boats; recommended for all water activities
5. **Boat Registration:** Required in West Virginia

## Additional Tips & Recommendations

### Best for Different Visitors

| Visitor Type | Best Time | Key Attractions |
|--------------|-----------|-----------------|
| **Families** | June-July | Swimming, rentals, lodge activities |
| **Fishing Enthusiasts** | April-May, Sept-Oct | Bass peak, saugeye, crappie |
| **Hunters** | Sept-Dec | Deer, turkey seasons; accessible WMA |
| **Backpackers/Hikers** | April-May, Sept-Oct | Trail system, scenic views |
| **Paddlers** | May-Sept | 82 miles shoreline, inlets, backwaters |
| **Photographers** | Fall (Sept-Nov) | Foliage, wildlife, light quality |

### Planning Your Visit

1. **Book camping 12 months in advance** for summer weekends
2. **Check season dates** - hunting and fishing seasons vary by month
3. **Reserve beach wheelchairs** 2+ weeks ahead if needed
4. **Bring cash** - not all facilities accept cards
5. **Plan for no cell service** - inform family of trip schedule
6. **Check water conditions** - winter drawdown minimal but worth confirming
7. **Bring life jackets** - legally required and essential for safety
8. **License requirements** - hunting, fishing licenses must be valid WV permits

### What to Bring

- WV fishing/hunting license (if applicable)
- Life jackets (required on boats)
- Cell phone (unreliable but bring for emergencies)
- Physical map of lake/WMA
- Emergency contact numbers written down
- Cash for small fees/rentals
- Sun protection (water reflects UV)
- Layered clothing (weather variable)
