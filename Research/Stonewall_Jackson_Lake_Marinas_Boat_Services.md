# Stonewall Jackson Lake Marinas & Boat Services

**Last Updated:** January 10, 2026

---

## 📍 Overview

Stonewall Jackson Lake is a 2,600-acre recreational reservoir located in central West Virginia near Weston, managed by the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers (Pittsburgh District) and operated by West Virginia State Parks. The lake offers comprehensive boating and watersports services through two main operators: **Stonewall Resort Marina** (primary) and **Invert Sports** (specialty watersports).

## 📋 BOATING REGULATIONS & SAFETY

### West Virginia Boating Laws (Applicable to Stonewall Jackson Lake)

#### Life Jacket (PFD) Requirements

| Category | Requirement |
|----------|-------------|
| **Children Under 13** | MUST WEAR PFD while underway (all vessel types) |
| **Adolescents 13+** | PFD must be readily available on board |
| **Adults** | PFD must be readily available (not required to wear) |
| **Personal Watercraft Operators** | MUST WEAR U.S. Coast Guard-approved Type I, II, III, or V PFD |
| **Water Sports Participants** | MUST WEAR PFD (anyone being towed) |
| **Inflatable PFDs** | NOT permitted for PWC use |

**PFD Specifications:** Must be U.S. Coast Guard-approved, in good condition, properly sized for wearer

#### Additional Safety Equipment

| Equipment | Vessel Type | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| **Type IV Throwable Device** | 16+ feet | Minimum one per vessel (required) |
| **Kayaks/Canoes 16+** | Paddle craft | EXEMPT from Type IV requirement |
| **Navigation Lights** | All motorboats (darkness hours) | Required for safe operation |
| **Whistle/Horn** | All vessels | Emergency signaling device |

#### Boater Education Certificate

- **Requirement:** Mandatory for anyone born on/after December 31, 1986
- **Certification:** NASBLA-approved Boater Education Course
- **Enforcement:** Must carry proof of completion
- **Available:** Online courses available through WVDNR

#### Vessel Registration

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| **Motorized Boats** | MUST have current title and registration |
| **Certificate of Number** | Required before operating motorboat |
| **Sailboats** | Must be registered if motorized |
| **Non-Motorized** | Kayaks, canoes (no motor) exempt |

#### Horsepower Limits

| Category | Limit |
|----------|-------|
| **Young Operators (12-15 years)** | Maximum 10 HP (without adult on board) |
| **Vessel Capacity Plate** | Maximum HP marked on plate must not be exceeded |
| **No Overpowering** | Safe powering capacity must be maintained |

**Note:** Stonewall Jackson Lake (government-owned USACE reservoir) does not have a blanket lake-wide horsepower limit. Operators must not exceed vessel manufacturer's rated horsepower.

#### No-Wake Zones

| Regulation | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| **Definition** | Areas where motorboats restricted from attaining wake-producing speed |
| **Enforcement** | Must operate at idling speed in marked no-wake zones |
| **Applicability** | Government-owned reservoirs with marked zones |
| **Stonewall Jackson Lake** | Several miles of no-wake zones established (see maps at lake office) |

**Personal Watercraft (PWC) Restrictions:**
- Cannot operate greater than slow/no-wake speed within 100 feet of:
  - Anchored or moored vessels
  - Shoreline, docks, piers, swim floats
  - Marked swim areas and swimmers
  - Persons angling or in manual vessels

#### Dam Safety Warning

**CRITICAL:** Warning horns sound before water release from powerhouses, sluiceways, or spillways.

- **Action Required:** IMMEDIATELY leave areas upstream and downstream of dam
- **Danger Level:** Even experienced boaters with powerful motors cannot safely escape spillway water flow
- **Recirculating Current:** Can pull boats upstream toward dam spillway downstream of structure
- **Fatality Risk:** High risk zone - do not linger near dam

## 🎣 FISHING & SPECIES

Stonewall Jackson Lake is known for multiple fish species and fishing opportunities:

- **Warm Water Species:** Largemouth bass, smallmouth bass, crappie, bluegill
- **Trout:** Stocked in select areas (check current stocking schedule)
- **Catfish:** Channel catfish throughout lake
- **Fishing Regulations:** Must comply with WV DNR fishing seasons and limits
- **License Required:** WV fishing license required (available online or at marina store)

**Note:** See WVDNR Fishing Regulations page for species-specific seasons and creel limits for Stonewall Jackson Lake.

## ✅ BOATING CHECKLIST FOR STONEWALL JACKSON LAKE

### Before You Go

- [ ] Current WV boating registration for motorized boats
- [ ] Boater Education Certificate (if born after 12/31/1986)
- [ ] Valid driver's license
- [ ] WV fishing license (if planning to fish)
- [ ] Check weather and lake conditions
- [ ] Verify marina hours (seasonal operation)
- [ ] Reserve rental equipment in advance (call 304-269-8895)

### Required Onboard Equipment

- [ ] Coast Guard-approved PFD for each person (properly sized)
- [ ] Type IV throwable device (if vessel 16+ feet)
- [ ] Navigation lights (for nighttime operation)
- [ ] Whistle or horn
- [ ] First aid kit
- [ ] Flashlight with extra batteries
- [ ] Fire extinguisher (class B or C for fuel systems)
- [ ] Lines and anchors

### Safety Precautions

- [ ] Wear PFD at all times (required for children under 13)
- [ ] Do not exceed vessel's rated horsepower
- [ ] Maintain slow/no-wake speed in marked zones
- [ ] Stay clear of dam area (listen for warning horns)
- [ ] File float plan with responsible party
- [ ] Carry communication device (cell phone, radio)
- [ ] Review no-wake zones on lake map before departure
- [ ] Know your vessel's capacity limits

### Personal Watercraft Operators

- [ ] U.S. Coast Guard-approved Type I, II, III, or V PFD (REQUIRED - must wear)
- [ ] No inflatable PFD substitutes
- [ ] Maintain slow/no-wake speed within 100 feet of shoreline
- [ ] No high-speed operation near swimmers or anchored vessels
- [ ] Boater Education Certificate

## 📊 SEASONAL NOTES

- **Peak Season:** May-September (warm water, full resort operations)
- **Spring:** Marina reopens April 2, 2026; water still cool
- **Summer:** Busiest period; make reservations well in advance
- **Fall:** Excellent boating weather; fewer crowds (September-October)
- **Winter:** Stonewall Resort Marina closes seasonally; limited services

**Weather Consideration:** Central WV experiences variable spring/fall weather and occasional flash flooding. Check forecast and water flow conditions before boating in tributary arms.

---

## 🚨 IMPORTANT SAFETY REMINDERS

1. **Dam Danger Zone:** Do not linger near the dam. Warning horns precede water releases. Spillway currents are lethal.

2. **Life Jackets Save Lives:** Children under 13 must wear PFD. All PWC operators must wear approved PFDs.

3. **No-Wake Zones:** Respect marked areas to protect other boaters, swimmers, and shoreline structures.

4. **Equipment Compliance:** Verify vessel horsepower rating and don't exceed manufacturer's limits.

5. **Boater Education:** Mandatory for operators born after 1986. Course completion required.

6. **Registration Required:** All motorized boats must be registered with WV DNR.
