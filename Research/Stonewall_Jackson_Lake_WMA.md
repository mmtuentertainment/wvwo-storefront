## Wildlife Management Area Overview

### Physical Characteristics

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Total Acreage** | 18,289 acres |
| **Lake Acreage** | 2,650 acres (Stonewall Jackson Lake) |
| **Terrain** | Farmlands reverting to woodland; rolling hills covered with mixed hardwood forest |
| **Forest Types** | Mixed hardwood forest (typical species: American beech, red maple, black cherry, sugar maple, yellow birch, cucumber magnolia, Northern red oak) |
| **Elevation Range** | Central WV plateau (specific range not documented in available sources) |

### Lake Features

- **Surface Area**: 2,650 acres
- **Primary Use**: Fishing, boating, water recreation
- **Boat Ramps**: 5 access points around the lake
- **Shooting Facility**: 300-yard rifle range (offseason use only)
- **Camping**: NOT permitted on the WMA (nearby Stonewall Resort State Park offers accommodations)

## Access Points & Roads

### Primary Access
- **Interstate 79**: Exits 91 and 96 (direct highway access)
- **Road Condition**: Generally passenger car accessible (specific road conditions should be verified with District 3 office)

### Boat Ramps
Five (5) boat ramps available around Stonewall Jackson Lake:
- Specific GPS coordinates for each ramp available on official WVDNR WMA map (11x17 PDF)
- Parking areas at each ramp

### Parking
- Parking available at boat ramp access points
- No comprehensive parking area count in available documentation

### ATV/UTV Access
- **General Rule**: ATVs and UTVs prohibited except where specifically permitted by posted signs on designated roads
- **Permitted Use**: Only on roads normally open to public travel and on designated routes (as posted)
- **Group Restrictions**: Groups of 10+ people require permit from District Wildlife Biologist or area manager
  - Maximum group size: 25 people
  - Prohibited during spring gobbler season
  - Prohibited October 10 - December 31 (except Sundays)

## Big Game Hunting Seasons & Regulations

### WHITE-TAILED DEER

#### Archery & Crossbow Season
- **Opening Date**: September 27, 2025
- **Urban Archery/Crossbow Split**: September 6 - December 31, 2025; January 12-31, 2026 (in incorporated cities/towns)
- **Bag Limit**: Up to 2 antlered bucks statewide (3 in CWD Containment Areas or with Class XS license)

#### Rifle Season
- **Buck Firearms Season**: Standard firearm season dates (specific dates vary by county - Lewis County included in general season)
- **Youth/Class Q/Class XS Antlerless Season**: October 18-19, December 26-27, 2025

#### Muzzleloader Season
- **Season Dates**: December 14-21, 2025
- **Note**: Lewis County OPEN (some counties are closed: Logan, McDowell, Mingo, Wyoming)

#### Mountaineer Heritage Season
- **Season Dates**: January 8-11, 2026
- **Availability**: Statewide

#### Antler Restrictions & Regulations
- **Maximum Harvest**: 2 antlered bucks per season
- **County-Specific Rule**: In certain counties (not including Lewis), hunters must harvest 1 antlerless deer before taking second antlered deer
- **Licensing**: Regular hunting license required (appropriate to method)

---

### WILD TURKEY

#### Spring Gobbler Season
- **Statewide Season**: April 20 - May 24, 2026
- **Youth Season**: April 18-19, 2026 (hunters under 18)
- **Method**: Shotgun, rifle (.22 centerfire minimum), or crossbow
- **Bag Limit**: 2 turkeys per season (regulations subject to county variations)

#### Fall Turkey Season
- **Primary Season**: October 11-19, 2025 (all 55 counties)
- **Extended Season Options**:
  - One additional week (October 27 - November 2): Not applicable to Lewis County
  - Three additional weeks (October 27 - November 16): Not applicable to Lewis County
- **Method**: Shotgun only
- **Bag Limit**: Antlerless birds only (regulations may vary)

---

### BLACK BEAR

#### Bear Firearms Season
- **Concurrent Antlerless Deer & Bear Season**: October 23-26, 2025 (41 counties or parts thereof - verify Lewis County inclusion)
- **Concurrent Buck Firearms & Bear Season**: During deer firearms season (specific dates vary by county)
- **Special Seasons**: September and October bear firearms seasons available (date specifics in full regulations document)

#### Bear Regulations
- **Bag Limit**: 1 bear per season
- **Licensing**: Bear hunting license required
- **Harvest Requirement**: All successful bear hunters must submit first premolar tooth to WVDNR by January 31 of following year
- **No Permit-Only Counties**: All bear seasons open to general hunting (2025)

## Small Game Hunting Seasons

### RUFFED GROUSE
- **Season Dates**: October 18 - February 28, 2025-2026
- **Daily Bag Limit**: 4 birds
- **Possession Limit**: 16 birds
- **Method**: Shotgun or .22 rimfire

### COTTONTAIL RABBIT
- **Season Dates**: November 1 - February 28, 2025-2026
- **Daily Bag Limit**: 5 rabbits
- **Possession Limit**: 20 rabbits
- **Method**: Shotgun, rifle (.22 rimfire or larger), or hunting dog

### SQUIRREL
- **Youth Season**: September 6-7, 2025
- **Regular Season**: September onward (full dates in regulations summary)
- **Daily Bag Limit**: 5 squirrels
- **Possession Limit**: 20 squirrels
- **Method**: .22 rimfire rifle or shotgun

## Furbearer Trapping Seasons

### BEAVER, MUSKRAT, RACCOON

| Species | Opening Date | Closing Date | Daily Check Required |
|---------|--------------|--------------|----------------------|
| Raccoon | November 4, 2025 | February 28, 2026 | Yes - All traps must be checked daily |
| Beaver | November 4, 2025 | February 28, 2026 | Yes - All traps must be checked daily |
| Muskrat | November 4, 2025 | February 28, 2026 | Yes - All traps must be checked daily |

#### General Trapping Regulations
- **Trap Setting**: Cannot be set before 8:00 a.m. on opening day
- **Daily Inspection**: Required for all species
- **Special Requirement**: Beaver, bobcat, fisher, and otter harvests must be electronically checked within 30 days of season close
- **Licensing**: Trapping license required

## WMA Regulations & Restrictions

### General Rules

| Rule | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Camping** | NOT permitted on WMA |
| **Vehicle Access** | ATVs/UTVs prohibited except on posted designated routes |
| **Group Size** | 10+ people require permit; maximum 25 people per group |
| **Group Restrictions** | Prohibited spring gobbler season & Oct 10-Dec 31 (except Sundays) |
| **Motorized Vehicles** | Allowed on roads normally open to public travel |
| **Hunting Hours** | Standard WV regulations apply (30 min. before sunrise to sunset, firearm specific) |
| **Orange Requirement** | Blaze orange requirements per WV regulations |

### Specific Prohibitions
- Hunting with bait (no bait sites allowed)
- ATV use in national forests or WMAs (with limited exceptions on designated roads)
- Night hunting (except predator hunting with proper licensing)
