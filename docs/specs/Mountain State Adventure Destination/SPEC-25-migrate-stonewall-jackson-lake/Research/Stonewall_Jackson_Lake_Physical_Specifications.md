# Stonewall Jackson Lake - Physical Specifications Research

**Research Completion Date:** January 10, 2026
**Destination Type:** Lake
**Location:** Lewis County, West Virginia
**Managing Agency:** U.S. Army Corps of Engineers (Pittsburgh District)

---

## Lake Basin Specifications

### Surface Area
- **Lake Size:** 2,650 acres (2,630-2,650 acres cited across sources)[^1][^2]
- **Drainage Area:** 101-102 square miles[^3][^4]
- **County:** Lewis County, West Virginia

### Shoreline
- **Shoreline Length:** 82 miles (80-82 miles cited across sources)[^1][^5]
- **Lake Length:** 26 miles[^5]

### Water Depth
- **Average Depth:** 18 feet[^2]
- **Maximum Depth:** 40 feet[^2]

---

## Dam Structure Specifications

### Physical Characteristics
- **Dam Type:** Concrete gravity dam with spillway and five sluices[^5]
- **Dam Length:** 620 feet[^5]
- **Dam Height:** 95 feet above stream[^5]
- **Construction Timeline:** Authorized under the Flood Control Act of 1962; construction completed and dam became fully operational in 1988[^5][^6]

### Water Level Management
- **Winter Drawdown:** Only 5 feet, resulting in minimal seasonal size variation[^5]
- **Normal Pool Elevation:** Data collection ongoing via USGS monitoring (vertical reference: NAVD88)[^4]

### Primary Dam Location
- **Primary Monitoring Station (USGS):** 39.0036° North, 80.4742° West[^4]

## Project Purpose & Operations

### Primary Purposes (Authorized Reasons for Impoundment)
- Flood control[^2][^10]
- Low flow augmentation for water quality[^2][^10]
- Water supply[^2][^10]
- Fish and wildlife enhancement[^2][^10]
- Hydropower[^2][^10]
- Recreation[^2][^10]

### Operational Status
- **Fully Operational:** 1988[^5]
- **Current Classification:** Flood control project of the Pittsburgh District, USACE[^2]

## Ecological & Recreational Significance

### Fishery Status
- **Designation:** West Virginia's No. 1 bass fishing lake[^13]
- **Fish Habitat:** Underwater structures (trees, roadbeds) from 1988 impoundment provide habitat for largemouth and smallmouth bass[^13]

### Recreation & Infrastructure
- **Wildlife Management Area:** 18,381 acres associated WMA[^5]
- **State Park Facilities:** Stonewall Resort State Park encompasses 1,833-1,900 acres with camping, lodging, and recreation facilities[^7][^11]
- **Visitors Center:** Located beneath Stonewall Jackson Dam[^13]

## Research Methodology Notes

This research compiled physical specifications from authoritative sources including:
- **U.S. Army Corps of Engineers** official project documentation and contact information
- **USGS National Water Information System** active monitoring data with NAVD88 elevation references
- **West Virginia Encyclopedia** for comprehensive lake specifications
- **West Virginia Division of Natural Resources** for fishery and recreational data
- **Recreation.gov and State Park Systems** for infrastructure and facility information

**Data Confidence Levels:**
- **High Confidence** (Primary USACE/USGS): Acreage, shoreline, average/maximum depth, dam specifications, GPS coordinates, drainage area
- **Moderate Confidence** (Verified across multiple sources): Dam completion year (1988), water quality and flood control purposes
- **Operational Data** (Continuous USGS monitoring): Real-time elevation data available at USGS Station 03057900

**Notable Variations Across Sources:**
- Lake acreage cited as 2,630-2,650 acres (likely reflects seasonal variation with 5-foot winter drawdown)
- Shoreline cited as 80-82 miles (variation likely due to measurement methodology at different water levels)
- All dam specifications (620-foot length, 95-foot height) consistent across authoritative sources
