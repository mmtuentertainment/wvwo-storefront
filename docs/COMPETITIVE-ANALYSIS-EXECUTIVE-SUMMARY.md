## WHAT COULD GO WRONG

### Risk 1: Competitors Copy Our Ideas
**Mitigation:** Speed and authenticity. AllTrails won't launch "hunting guides." OnX won't become friendly. We maintain our moat through consistent execution.

### Risk 2: We Lose Authenticity While Scaling
**Mitigation:** Kim's voice baked into every new destination guide. Never corporate. Community feedback keeps us honest.

### Risk 3: SEO Authority Gap Widens
**Mitigation:** 250 destinations + quality backlinks + Google Search Console optimization + content marketing. DR 55 is achievable in 12 months with discipline.

### Risk 4: Outfitter Partnerships Don't Materialize
**Mitigation:** Start with local guides (Gauley River guides, lake resorts). Build affiliate network second (Amazon, REI). Commissions model doesn't require partnerships to work.

### Risk 5: Native App Development Fails
**Mitigation:** Web-first strategy works fine through 2027. Native app is Phase 4 (2027+), not critical to 2026 victory.
